      How review works

      Fast intuition. Slow reasoning. One agent system.

      Our proprietary agent system combines two kinds of thinking. System 1 models judge thousands of options in milliseconds. LLM agents reason through the few that matter. Deterministic tools then check the result.

      System 1 models

      Fast, trained intuition. Purpose-built models trained on plans, quantities and cost data score a layout the way an experienced planner glances at it: instantly. They sweep through every grid, core position and facade ratio and keep only the promising ones.

      LLM agents

      Slow, deliberate reasoning. Specialised agents read briefs, zoning plans and stakeholder files, weigh trade-offs, explain their choices and coordinate the work. They decide which ideas the System 1 models should chase next.
