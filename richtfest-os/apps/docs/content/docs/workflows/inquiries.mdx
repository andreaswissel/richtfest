---
title: Inquiries
description: Questions to the architect, client or authorities, bundled with every email, call and document exchanged about them.
---

An inquiry is a ticket for one question to a stakeholder. It collects the request as sent and everything that comes back: emails, call notes or transcripts, documents and internal notes. Open **Inquiries** in the project bar.

## Create an inquiry

- **Component data**: in the building model, open **Sound insulation**, enter who you ask under **Data request** and choose **Create inquiry**. Richtfest stores the request text and the items it asks for (per wall role and ceilings) and sets the inquiry to **Waiting for reply**. Send the text yourself, for example with **Copy request**; Richtfest does not send email.
- **Traffic data**: in **Exterior noise**, choose the sources under **Request traffic data**, enter the authority and choose **Create inquiry**. Only inputs an authority or operator supplies are asked for; progress follows the values entered on the sources.
- **General**: in **Inquiries**, choose **New inquiry** with a subject, the counterpart and optionally the request text you sent.

## Add the exchange

Under **Add entry**, choose **Email**, **Call**, **Document** or **Note**, whether it was **Received** or **Sent**, and paste the text: an email body, call notes or a transcript. Optionally attach a project file (upload it in Sources first). The date defaults to now. Entries appear in the timeline in the order they happened.

To file an email without copying it, export it from the mail program (`.eml` from Outlook for Mac, Apple Mail, Thunderbird or webmail; `.msg` from classic Outlook for Windows) and choose **Import email (.eml, .msg)**. Richtfest fills subject, time and text, sets **Received** unless the sender shares your email domain, and stores the original email and its attachments in Sources, where they get a document type. A PDF attachment is linked to the entry, otherwise the email itself. Check the filled entry, then add it.

Status follows the exchange: something sent sets **Waiting for reply**, something received sets **Answered**, notes change nothing. Close an inquiry when it is done; a closed inquiry stays closed until reopened.

## Forwarding address

Each project can get a forwarding address. In **Inquiries**, choose **Create forwarding address**; it stays the same for the project, so it can be saved in an address book. Every inquiry also shows its own **Reply address**, the project address with a short suffix.

- CC an inquiry's reply address when you send the request. The architect's reply-all, and anything else sent or forwarded to that address, is filed into the inquiry automatically and marked **Forwarded**. Your own CC counts as sent and sets **Waiting for reply**; mail from outside counts as received.
- Mail to the project address lands in the **Inbox** above the inquiry list. Choose the inquiry and **File email**, or **Dismiss** it.
- The original email and its attachments are always kept in Sources, also when dismissed. A PDF attachment is linked to the entry so **Read component data** reads it.

Richtfest never sends email from these addresses. Anyone who knows an address can send to it, so filed mail is shown with its sender, and component values from it stay proposals until reviewed. Setup is described in [deployment](/docs/engineering/deployment#inbound-email).

## Read component data from a reply

For component data inquiries, **Read component data** on a received entry reads the reply the same way as a construction description, for example "Außenwände: Kalksandstein 36,5 cm, RDK 1,8". Quoted earlier messages ("> …", "Am … schrieb …", "-----Original Message-----") are skipped, so Richtfest's own question is never read back as an answer. An attached document's pages are read as well.

Values become unreviewed proposals for every wall of that role or the ceilings, citing the inquiry entry (and the page, for an attached document). They never replace entered or reviewed values. In the data request they are labelled "per reply" until confirmed.

## Follow-up

While items are open, a component data inquiry shows a **Follow-up**: the current request limited to what is still missing or unconfirmed, starting with a thank-you for the reply. Values the counterpart already stated in a reply or its attachment are not asked again; review them in the building model instead. **Copy follow-up** copies the text for an email, and **Log as sent** adds it to the timeline as a sent email, which sets **Waiting for reply**.

## Progress

A component data inquiry shows how many of its items are answered and which are still open. An item counts as answered once every requested wall or ceiling has a reviewed value for it, however the value was entered. The building model's **Sound insulation** popover lists open inquiries with their progress, and agents receive them in the building-model context with a link to the inquiry.
