---
title: Building geometry
description: Canonical rooms and walls in a provisional, navigable whole-building view.
---

In a project's **Building model**, switch from **2D** to **3D** to see available
floors and wall footprints in the selected building and construction state. Canonical rooms and usable
extraction drafts appear together, using the same persisted IDs, outlines, holes
and metric plan scale as the 2D overlays. There is no separate visualization
model to synchronize. Edit outlines in 2D; edit heights in the 3D dimension panel.

## Select PDF pages and review coverage

The source selector chooses a document revision; page numbers refer to that PDF,
not to building floors. Documents with more than twelve pages use a compact page
selector. Non-plan, unresolved and not-yet-registered pages remain viewable without
creating storeys or guessing geometry. Selecting an object opens its own source page.

**Plan context → PDF page coverage** lists each source separately, with detected
floor-plan pages, non-plan pages and unresolved pages. The known PDF page count is
not the registration count: when the count is unknown, only actual registered or
classified page numbers are shown. A truncated extraction remains explicitly marked.
Source descriptions and plan-set replacement controls have separate rows; replacing
a plan set still requires an explicit choice and action.

## Navigate floors and inspect the enclosure

**All floors** shows the provisional stack. Without saved site alignment it is the initial view. Select a floor in the linked floor
list, or click an object in the overview, to glide closer and fade the other
floors. Hover or keyboard-focus a floor link to highlight its outline without moving
the camera. Click the focused floor again, click empty canvas, or use **All floors**
to restore the overview. When inspecting a room, its floor link first returns to the
whole floor. Faded objects cannot intercept selection;
reduced-motion preferences make both transitions immediate. Floor links retain
the source page and can be opened in another tab. **Review outline in 2D** returns
to that floor's source plan and selected object.

Automatically created floors display their entered level or a consistent floor
title read from active plan sources, rather than “Plan page N”. Custom names are
preserved. Missing or conflicting identities display **Unidentified floor**.
The inspector quotes the source title as **unreviewed**. Displaying that title
does not rename stored records, assign a level, approve geometry or change calculations.

When all displayed storeys have level indices and one is level **0**, a translucent
surface marks an **assumed ground reference** there. Negative storeys such as −1
and −2 sit below it and remain visible and selectable through it. This is a schematic
surface, not surveyed terrain, a slab or a calculation input. Missing levels do not
become basement levels from PDF page order; without the required indices the ground
surface is hidden and the panel explains why. A deep link to a focused floor keeps
that focus instead of opening the overview.

Walls use actual source polygons, including holes, and the storey's clear height.
Generic construction fills and fragment groups are not silently treated as walls.
Rendering a wall is not confirmation of its exterior role, phase, fire resistance
or a complete, weather-tight building envelope.

**Assumed floor alignment and spacing** explains the temporary placement: floor
plans retain their shared metric sheet coordinates, with a single common origin.
Smaller upper floors keep their offsets instead of being independently centred.
Registration between drawings is still unverified. Entered levels determine order; missing levels use plan
page order. Vertical spacing uses the greatest room/storey clear height on the
lower floor plus a **0.30 m assumed floor allowance**, not a measured slab.
These transforms are display-only and never become engineering inputs. The view
uses one primary plan per storey, preferring the current plan set, and excludes
superseded revisions, other buildings and pages classified as sections/elevations.
It does not merge alternative drawings or stitch multiple partial plans together.

## Present the building and furnished cutaways

**Building** assembles opaque plaster walls, framed opening infills, room finishes,
illustrative furniture, slabs and horizontal roof caps from the same outlines.
Select a floor to reveal its furnished **Cutaway**; other floors fade, stop
intercepting clicks and stop casting shadows over its interior. Toggle **Cutaway**
off to see the selected floor at full height. Drag to orbit, use **Top view** for
the floor layout, or **Expand view** to hide the review panel. Construction-state
filters still apply: use **Proposed** for the intended design, not **Existing**.

Floor changes animate the camera, cutaway and fading together. Select a room in
the model or room list to fly into its source-outline bounds and inspect its source and
height assumptions. The floor link returns from the room to the entire floor;
**All floors** restores the complete building.

When the model has a saved site placement matching its plan revision, page and
coordinate system, **Surroundings** is the initial view. It reuses the project's
recorded neighbouring buildings, trees and official aerial imagery, with source
attribution. **All floors** moves closer to the building; selecting a floor fades
the surroundings as well as the other floors. Tree detections remain unreviewed,
not surveyed vegetation. Where the regional provider supplies no trees, none are
invented. An unavailable aerial image does not block model inspection.
In **Building** presentation, contextual building masses overlapping the model's
footprint are hidden so they do not cover the interior. This is only a display
mask, not a declaration that the buildings are the same object or demolished;
their source records are unchanged.

An address alone is not a plan alignment. Without a matching saved placement the
viewer stays schematic and links to **3D location** for site setup, rather than
centering the building on a guessed address. Site placement does not verify the
registration between floor drawings or approve engineering inputs.

**Source geometry** removes presentation additions and shows the underlying
wall and translucent room geometry. **Presentation assumptions** lists what was
added. Canonical opening geometry takes precedence over traced gaps; saved
opening heights are used where available. Other traced gaps are only illustrative
infills, not confirmed windows, wall hosts or escape-route connections. Their
default glazing is 1.40 m tall with a 0.90 m sill, or a 2.10 m door where a door
swing was traced, shortened as necessary to fit the storey.

Furniture and finishes are illustrative and are never saved as surveyed facts.
Furniture is omitted when it cannot fit inside a room, including its holes.
Slabs and roof caps follow the union of available outlines without bridging
separate blocks or courtyards. Roof caps do **not** represent verified pitched
roofs. Missing roof profiles, balconies, unclassified openings and incomplete
extraction still need review; this is a provisional presentation, not an as-built
or construction-ready model. None of these display additions enters calculations.

## Start from extracted outlines

In **Source geometry**, usable room drafts appear immediately in amber, even before any room has been
accepted. Opening 3D neither creates duplicate rooms nor approves the extraction.
Opening 3D does not enable draft geometry in the normal assessment workflow. The
separate, explicitly unreviewed candidate-calculation preview described below can
use it for conditional calculations without accepting or approving it.

Select a draft volume or its linked row. **Review outline in 2D** opens the same
object on the source plan for inspection and correction. **Accept room outline**
uses the existing review workflow to create its canonical room, then opens that
room's height editor. It does not confirm the height, construction phase, room
use or topology. A suggested match to an existing room must be resolved in 2D
instead of creating another room. Rejected drafts disappear from the preview.

Drafts inherit a storey height or use the unsaved 2.60 m preview value. A storey
default can be edited immediately; room-specific heights are saved after accepting
the room outline. Draft room phases remain unconfirmed, as in the 2D review view.

## Complete and confirm heights

1. Select a room in the volume view or its linked row. **Needs confirmation**
   includes draft outlines, rooms that cannot be rendered and unreviewed heights.
2. Choose **Storey default** to set the clear height inherited by rooms in that
   storey, or **Room override** for a different height in one room.
3. **Save assumed height** appends an inferred, unreviewed property version.
   Review the saved value separately with **Confirm saved height**, or reject it.
   **Use storey height** clears an override and restores inheritance.

Without either value, the preview uses **2.60 m**. Merely opening 3D does not save
that default or approve anything. Amber volumes have draft outlines or unreviewed
heights; neutral canonical volumes have reviewed heights, not necessarily reviewed topology.
Source evidence, inferred provenance, prior reviewed values and later edits stay
separate. Confirming an assumed height does not turn it into a source measurement.

## Read building heights from plans

Open **Fire protection → Section and elevation heights → Extract heights** for an enrolled PDF revision.
New automatic plan imports also queue this pass. It reads up to six identified
section/elevation pages, not every page or historical upload. Omitted pages,
unavailable providers and uncertain references remain visible gaps.

Expand a page to inspect its printed dimensions, floor and terrain elevations,
ridge/eaves heights and possible fire-protection height. The receipt retains the
source revision/page/quote, provider, model, preset, usage and input hash. Quotes
and numbers must occur in the supplied page text; their meaning, units, endpoints
and model-proposed regions still require visual review. A proposed region is not
a verified crop and is not copied into canonical height evidence.

Height reading uses the active provider's low preset and honors its configured
output-token budget and per-call timeout, rather than imposing the ordinary
document indexer's 3,000-token cap. Reasoning and the final JSON share that token
budget. For dense sections, configure sufficient headroom in Agent model settings;
the application does not silently increase a saved limit or switch models.
Receipts record effective settings and prompt/instruction hashes. Version 2
collapses PDF layout whitespace before checking the 500-character quote limit,
while retaining literal source matching. It also asks the reader to trace both
dimension endpoints and distinguish terrain markers from floor levels. These
instructions do not guarantee correct interpretation or complete coverage.

Only a height explicitly relating the highest habitable floor to mean terrain,
with known units and floor basis, can be transferred to an empty building height.
Transfer saves an **inferred, unreviewed** property; it neither overwrites an
existing value nor approves it for classification. Confirm the saved property
separately after checking the original page and applicable height definition.
Roof height, an isolated elevation and a printed dimension with unknown endpoints
are not substitutes. This pass does not infer Sonderbau status.

Completed runs are retained rather than silently repeated. Explicit retries are
available only when every attempted page stopped before provider execution
(unconfigured, forbidden or unavailable). Failed or interrupted calls with an
unknown provider outcome are not automatically replayed.

## Calculation boundary

Exterior-noise facade areas use the canonical room override before the storey
height. New inferred heights do not enter calculations until explicitly reviewed;
the unsaved 2.60 m preview never enters calculations. Existing source/manual draft
inputs retain their existing draft-calculation behavior.

Interior sound currently supports one uniform height per storey. Unequal room
heights, or an unconfirmed inferred height, leave height-dependent checks
incomplete instead of silently substituting the storey default. This first
workflow does not implement unequal-height junction calculations.

## Calculate before engineering approval

Open **Plan context → Candidate calculations** on a completed PDF floor-plan
extraction. The read-only preview combines that page's canonical geometry with
usable unreviewed room and wall candidates. It derives provisional connections,
room areas and openings, then runs the existing fire and interior-sound assemblers.
Rejected connections and properties stay rejected; confirmed values take precedence.
Missing heights, materials, densities and performance ratings are not defaulted.

The preview can calculate conditional first-route lengths when roles, geometry,
scale and connections are supplied; it does not require geometry approval first.
Missing exits, ambiguous connections, unsupported holes or IFC routes stay blocked.
Exterior noise currently reports geometry and missing inputs, not a complete
facade calculation. Cross-storey flanks, slabs and whole-building coverage remain
outside this page-scoped preview.

The result is explicitly **unreviewed**, even when a check can be evaluated.
Download its JSON receipt for the source revision/page, candidate IDs, classifier
receipts, extraction version, model revision, input hash, results and omissions.
It does not write candidates, canonical entities, properties, reviews or a model
revision. A new source/page or model revision clears the displayed result.

**Accept room outline** remains a separate source-identity/geometry decision, not
engineering approval. Area agreement alone is insufficient for automatic acceptance:
check the source boundary, holes, connected spaces and area convention first.
Reference-report input reproduction is a separate evaluation lane; matching its
printed answers does not independently validate plan extraction or project compliance.

## Scope of the preview

The view shows a provisional room-and-wall stack, not a complete BIM authoring system.
Missing metric scale or usable room outlines prevent rendering. The empty state
distinguishes no rooms, missing scale and unusable outlines. **Set plan scale**
opens Plan context when the metric scale is missing. Label anchors, invalid
polygons and unclassified construction footprints are not extruded; unresolved rooms
stay in the review list. Floors with missing geometry or scale remain listed.

Opening cut-outs, slabs, roofs, verified floor registration, IFC mesh editing and
direct vertex dragging in 3D are not part of this workflow. It neither invents topology nor assigns
fire resistance, acoustic ratings, classifications or escape-route connections.
Those remain separate source-backed, explicitly reviewed inputs.
