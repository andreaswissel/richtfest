---

A fire protection assessment starts from two variables: the federal state (its Landesbauordnung, LBO) and the building class (Gebäudeklasse, GK). Everything a concept needs for a residential building is in the LBO and the VVTB; DIN 4102 and DIN EN 13501 only come in for the classification of individual components. Richtfest prepares the Nachweis or Brandschutzkonzept in the building model, on the same walls, rooms and doors that sound insulation uses. The fire protection specialist reviews and signs it.

Two states are captured so far: **Rheinland-Pfalz** (LBauO RLP as amended on 19 Nov 2025, GarStellVO RLP of 8 Dec 2022) and **Berlin** (BauO Bln as amended on 9 Jul 2026). Other states show "State not covered" instead of falling back to the Musterbauordnung, because each state adopts it differently. For example, RLP has no 400 m² unit limit for GK 4, while Berlin has one.

## Classify the building

Open the project's **Fire protection** tab, next to **Building model**, and enter:

- **Federal state** of the building.
- **Height of highest habitable floor**: from the ground surface (on average) to the finished floor of the highest storey in which an Aufenthaltsraum is *possible* (§ 2 Abs. 2 LBauO, § 2 Abs. 3 BauO Bln), as dimensioned in the section. An attic counts when a window could easily be added.
- **Dwellings / other units**: enter the complete unit schedule. Detected apartment names remain a provisional hint, not a complete count for classification; missing other units do not default to zero. Empty inputs link candidate source documents and explain the evidence needed.
- Berlin only: the **largest unit** and **all units together**, as Brutto-Grundfläche. Between 7 and 13 m, a building is GK 4 only if every unit has at most 400 m²; otherwise it is GK 5. GK 1 and 2 need at most two units with 400 m² in total.
- **Sonderbau** (§ 50 LBauO, § 2 Abs. 4 BauO Bln), **garage usable area and type** (GarStellVO: up to 100 m² small, up to 1,000 m² medium, above that large), and how the **Treppenraum is vented**. When buildings on one site vent differently (Bendenweg: 6a with windows, 6b with an opening at the top), set the venting on each Treppenraum room as well.

Richtfest then shows the building class, whether it is a Hochhaus or Sonderbau, and what has to be delivered:

| Case | Deliverable | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GK 1–3, no Sonderbau | Brandschutznachweis: colour-coded plans | None |
| GK 4, GK 5 or Sonderbau | Brandschutzkonzept: plans plus written part | RLP: Prüfsachverständige/r für Brandschutz (four-eyes principle). Berlin: Prüfingenieur/in für Brandschutz |

In Berlin, the fire protection is also checked for a Mittel- or Großgarage (§ 66 Abs. 3 BauO Bln). Berlin has no Garagenverordnung, so the garage size classes follow the Muster-Garagenverordnung and the draft leaves the garage requirements as a placeholder.

The 7 m threshold between GK 3 and GK 4 is the reach of the fire brigade's ladder; it is the biggest jump in requirements. A Mittel- or Großgarage adds the garage chapter to the written part but does not require a concept by itself. Missing inputs are listed instead of guessed.

## Brandschutzplan

**Show Brandschutzplan** in the Fire protection tab opens the building model with the fire overlay enabled. Choose **Plan page for escape routes** to set the page used for the route summary and written draft; this is not a whole-building route assessment. The tab uses the same saved properties, review controls and source-height observations as the building model. The existing toolbar popover and layers button remain available while working on the plan.

The overlay colours the plan the way the office draws it (ARCenergie legend):

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Magenta, hatched | fb+A+M: Bauart einer Brandwand |
| Red | fb: feuerbeständig (90 min) |
| Orange, hatched | hf+M: hochfeuerhemmend, also under mechanical load (Treppenraum walls in GK 4) |
| Orange | hf: hochfeuerhemmend (60 min) |
| Yellow | fh: feuerhemmend (30 min) |
| Brown | nb: nichtbrennbar, without fire resistance (non-load-bearing facade of a Treppenraum) |
| Dark green | Notwendiger Treppenraum: the safest area |
| Light green | Notwendiger Flur |
| Door tag | D, DS, V-DS, RS, T30-DS/RS, T90-DS/RS |
| Green line and tag | Escape route of a room with its length; red above 35 m |

Each wall's requirement follows from the rooms on both sides: Trennwände between units or towards other common areas (§ 29), walls of notwendige Flure (§ 35) and of the notwendiger Treppenraum (§ 34). The strictest requirement wins, except that walls of a notwendiger Flur or Treppenraum follow their own rule instead of § 29. In GK 4, a dwelling's wall to the notwendiger Flur is therefore feuerhemmend and a wall between dwellings hochfeuerhemmend, as in the office's plans. A Treppenraum's own facade wall is marked nichtbrennbar (§ 34 Abs. 6 Satz 2) unless it is load-bearing. A stairwell is a notwendiger Treppenraum and a common corridor a notwendiger Flur from GK 3; set **Escape role** on a room to override this. Doors take the closure their own rule requires, for example RS from the Treppenraum into a notwendiger Flur (§ 34 Abs. 9).

Berlin differs in a few rules: Trennwände take the fire resistance of the storey's load-bearing walls (at least feuerhemmend) and are not required in GK 1 and 2 Wohngebäude; Treppenraum walls in GK 3 are feuerhemmend; basement walls between Aufenthaltsräume and other rooms are Trennwände; a smoke opening at the top of a Treppenraum does not make its doors T30-RS; and doors from a notwendiger Flur to basement storage are T30-DS. Paragraph numbers follow the BauO Bln (Treppenräume § 35, Flure § 36). Slabs (§ 31) appear in the written part, not on the plan.

Select a wall, slab or door to see its requirement with the cited paragraphs. Enter whether it is load-bearing, the **provided fire resistance** (or closure) and its basis, for example "Stahlbeton 20 cm, DIN 4102-4". The status then reads met, not met or open. Not-met components are outlined in red on the plan.

Escape route lengths follow § 34 Abs. 2: the shortest walking line from the farthest point of each Aufenthaltsraum (and basement room) to a Treppenraum door or outside. The line goes around walls through doors, and furniture is ignored. It is drawn on the plan like the office's Brandschutzpläne, with its length at the farthest point. They need the plan scale and reviewed doors, and stay indicative until both are confirmed.

## Written part

**Copy written part (draft)** produces the text of the Brandschutzkonzept in German. For each requirement, it quotes the LBO text verbatim and then states how the building meets it. Each statement is built from the components' provided ratings, so material information such as "load-bearing walls are reinforced concrete" still comes from the user. Unmet or open requirements are collected as deviations (Abweichungen) for the specialist. The fire water demand (DVGW W 405, AGBF 2018-4: 48 m³/h for buildings with up to three Vollgeschosse, otherwise 96 m³/h, for 2 hours) is a summary, not a quote, and must be confirmed against the source.

Until the Brandschutzatlas structure is available, the draft follows the chapters of the office's Bendenweg concept (1 Allgemeine Angaben to 7 Anlagen). It fills what the model knows: class, Sonderbau, garage, fire water, escape route lengths, Treppenraum, Flur, wall, slab and door requirements, and smoke venting per Treppenraum. Everything else is left as a bracketed placeholder, for example the Vorgespräch, fire brigade areas and building services. A chapter closes with "Alle Anforderungen werden eingehalten." only when every component it covers is met. Every draft is unreviewed; edit and check it before issuing.

## Ask the Richtfest Agent for a draft

After preparing the saved building model, choose **Team** and ask for a Brandschutz draft. The requirements reviewer or construction-law specialist can call `prepare_fire_protection_assessment` with no numeric arguments. It reads the saved model revision, runs the deterministic checks and saves a **Brandschutz · Teilstand · Entwurf** artifact with source links, open items and the complete calculation receipt. It does not approve the model or certify compliance. Chat stays conversational and cannot create this draft. Report and Calculation currently use acoustic templates, not a Brandschutz workflow.

The agent path deliberately requires explicit saved room escape roles and unit/common-space membership rather than reproducing the plan view's inferred defaults. Approved properties take precedence over pending suggestions; unreviewed manual entries are flagged. Unresolved conflicts are not calculation inputs. Routes require one registered PDF plan per storey, confirmed numeric scale, reviewed alignment and approved door/wall connections. Missing connections, unknown basement levels and polygons with holes block route calculations instead of being guessed. Several active buildings must be assessed separately; they are not combined into one classification.

The tool requires project-read and knowledge-write access. Team specialists need at least two model steps and all of the snapshot's sources in the current readable batch. Retries reuse the saved result and artifact; after changing the building model, start a new run. The artifact retains model revision, code editions, engine version and input/output hashes; run exports retain the requested and reported AI model identities. See [Building assessment backtests](/docs/engineering/building-backtests) for comparison against withheld references. An upload alone is not yet an automated, reviewed assessment.

## Not yet supported

- States other than Rheinland-Pfalz and Berlin.
- The Brandschutzplan and escape routes on [IFC-imported](/docs/workflows/project-onboarding#import-an-ifc-model) rooms and elements; they are drawn on PDF plan pages.
- Compensation proposals for deviations. Deviations are detected only.
- Second escape route and fire brigade access (Anleiterstellen, Rettungswegfenster) on the plan.
- GarStellVO component rules beyond the garage classification.
- PDF export of the Brandschutzplan.
- AHO fee calculation from the plan areas.
